Something you know

Password + TOTP

Phishable in real time. Replay protection depends on the verifier enforcing a monotonic counter. Recovery usually collapses to email.

  • Not origin-bound
  • Codes can be relayed
  • Works everywhere
Synced credential

Synced passkey

Origin-bound WebAuthn credential, end-to-end encrypted by the platform sync fabric. Device loss is survivable; cloud-account compromise becomes the recovery single point of failure.

  • Phishing-resistant
  • Survives device loss
  • Sync account is a SPOF
Something you have

Hardware FIDO2 key

Origin-bound, non-exportable key material and a monotonic signature counter. No sync: loss requires a second key or a recovery code.

  • Phishing-resistant
  • Clone detection
  • Device loss is permanent
